## 2024-05-18 - Form Accessibility in Admin Views
**Learning:** Proper form associations (`htmlFor` mapped to input `id`) are especially critical for applications with extensive administrative tasks or heavy form usage. Without them, interfaces may look polished visually but fail basic functional expectations for accessibility.
**Action:** Always ensure that every `<label>` correctly associates with its corresponding `<input>` to provide an intuitive and accessible UX, especially in complex admin views.
